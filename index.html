Easy (E) — recovery runs, aerobic base, long runs. Conversational effort.
Marathon (M) — goal marathon race pace; sustainable for ~2–3 hours.
Threshold (T) — "comfortably hard" tempo runs or cruise intervals; improves lactate clearance.
Interval (I) — hard effort in the 3–5 min-per-rep range; builds aerobic power (VO2max).
Repetition (R) — short, fast reps (≤2 min) with full recovery; builds speed and running economy.
